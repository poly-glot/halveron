Our capabilities

Halveron is a consultancy that does one thing: we help service organisations run their operations well. Our consultants have managed contact centres, claims teams, back offices and frontline services themselves, so we know the difference between a plan that reads well and one that works on a wet Monday in January. We bring the same few disciplines to every engagement: start with customer demand, design the work before the technology, and leave your people able to carry on without us.

  • Business transformation

    Transformation succeeds when the destination is clear, each stage pays back early and someone owns every benefit. We help leadership teams plan, govern and deliver change that customers and staff notice for the right reasons.

    View more about Business transformation

  • Operations strategy and implementation

    Your operations strategy decides much of what you spend and how customers feel about you. We connect it to choices about capacity, channels, locations and sourcing, then help you put those choices into practice.

    View more about Operations strategy and implementation

  • Operating model design

    An operating model is how your organisation turns strategy into service every day. We design structures, roles, processes and systems around what customers need, and test the design before anyone moves desk.

    View more about Operating model design

  • Organisational effectiveness

    Good people in a poorly designed organisation spend their days working around it. We look at structure, decision rights and management routines, and help you make good work easier to do.

    View more about Organisational effectiveness

  • Multichannel customer experience

    Customers move between phone, web, app and letter without a second thought. We map their real journeys, find where they break and redesign them so each contact moves the customer forward.

    View more about Multichannel customer experience

  • Process improvement

    Most service processes carry work that customers never asked for: rework, chasing and duplicate checks. We use lean and systems thinking, with your own teams, to remove it and keep it out.

    View more about Process improvement

  • Digital at pace

    We design digital services around redesigned work, not old processes. Working with your technology teams and selected partners, we prototype and test with real customers in weeks, then help you build what works.

    View more about Digital at pace

  • Learning and development

    Lasting improvement depends on people who can lead it. Our accredited open courses, custom programmes and on-the-job coaching build practical skills in lean, service design and operational management, and leave your teams able to lead change themselves.

    View more about Learning and development
